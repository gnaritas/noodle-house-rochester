Rochester’s new home for noodles.

The Noodle House serves the bowls Chef Chai and Marlene Rachwat never had room for on the menu at Mango Thai a few doors up Broadway: pho, khao soi, ramen and chicken karaage, cooked in the 1858 Kelley Building on the corner of South Broadway and Fourth Street SE.

Before you come

The questions people ask.

When did The Noodle House open?

The restaurant soft-opened on Sunday, November 8, 2026, from noon to 8 p.m. — the owners’ own announcement, reported by the Post Bulletin and KROC News. It is open seven days a week.

What’s on the menu?

The noodle dishes the owners couldn’t fit on the menu at their Mango Thai restaurant: pho, khao soi, ramen and chicken karaage — the dishes Chef Chai and Marlene Rachwat told KROC News they would serve. The full menu, with prices, is at the restaurant.

When are you open?

Seven days a week: 11 a.m. to 9 p.m. Monday through Thursday, 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. Friday, noon to 10 p.m. Saturday, noon to 8 p.m. Sunday — hours as published in the restaurant’s own Instagram bio.

Where exactly are you?

At 332 Broadway Avenue South in the Kelley Building — Rochester’s first brick building, on the corner of South Broadway and Fourth Street SE downtown, a few doors south of Mango Thai.

Is it related to Mango Thai?

Yes — Chef Chai and Marlene Rachwat, who run Mango Thai a few doors up Broadway at 318 S. Broadway, opened The Noodle House as its own restaurant, dedicated entirely to the noodle dishes they love.

Find the corner of Broadway and 4th.

The Kelley Building holds the whole corner — and we’re in the front of it, a few doors south of Mango Thai.
