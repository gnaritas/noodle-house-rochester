Privacy

Plain words about your data.

Last updated November 22, 2026. This website is published for The Noodle House. It sets no cookies of its own, runs no third-party scripts, and keeps no analytics — pages are plain text and one stylesheet.

The note form

When you submit the contact form, the name, phone, email and message you type are sent to the restaurant’s inbox through an email-delivery service, so a person can answer. That is the entire purpose. Nothing is sold, rented, or added to a mailing list.

Maps

Pages with a map load a Google Maps embed, which may set Google’s own cookies once you look at it. Google’s privacy policy covers what Google does with those.

Your choices

Want a note deleted after it’s answered? Reply to the email you receive and say so.
